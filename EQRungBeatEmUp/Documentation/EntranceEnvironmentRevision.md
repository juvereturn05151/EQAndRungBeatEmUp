# Entrance Gate extended scenery and breakable props

Stage01_EntranceGate uses a 720×320 background and 720×480 floor. The revised background removes the painted portable metal barricades and parked motorcycle, and adds the complete upper gate crown, scaffold, trees and night sky. The floor extends downward with pavement detail and fairground reflections; the original upper 240 RGBA rows are retained exactly. Stage art remains 12.8 world units wide and covers approximately Y -2.738 through 7.328. The background and floor overlap slightly to avoid an exposed seam.

Six independent `EntranceFence` prefabs sit at X -5.45, -3.8, -2.15, 2.15, 3.8 and 5.45, all at lane Y 0.6. Each takes three accepted hits. One `EntranceMotorcycle` prefab sits at (4.65, -0.05) and takes six accepted hits. Both use the existing `DestructibleObject` and `CombatWall` systems: trigger hurtboxes, small ground blockers, damaged and broken sprites, metal impact/break sounds, reused combat VFX, timed destruction states and scattered debris. Both colliders disable on destruction; stage restart restores the original props. The center of the gate stays clear and the fence sections can be approached or walked around from the lower lane.

The new props are authored in the Entrance Gate destructibles list in `Assets/EQ_Rung_BeatEmUp/Levels/HauntedHouse/ThaiHauntedHouse.asset`. Existing boxes, jars, encounters, stage order, movement bounds and entrance/exit points are preserved. Prefabs are in `Assets/EQ_Rung_BeatEmUp/Prefabs/Props/`, with twelve transparent state/debris sprites and two metallic SFX in `Assets/EQ_Rung_BeatEmUp/ArtAssets/Props/Destructibles/`. The multiplayer asset catalog includes the new sprite states and a refreshed content hash.

`Tools/EntranceRevision/` retains the built-in ImageGen prompt set, generated prop sheets, original environment backups and repeatable packaging script. The setup menu **Beat Em Up → Stages → Configure extended Entrance Gate art and props** updates this stage and these two prefabs. The existing prop import setup now derives its ground pivot from each sprite's actual height, so rebuilding box/jar defaults also preserves the taller fence/motorcycle sprite pivots.

Validation menus: **Beat Em Up → Stages → Validate extended Entrance Gate coverage** renders the art and authored props in a disposable preview scene. **Beat Em Up → Props → Validate destructible props (Play Mode)** exercises boxes, jars, fences and motorcycle with real attack sampling, repeated-hit deduplication, hitstop, breaking, debris, collision removal and stage restart. Results are in `EntranceEnvironmentValidationResults.txt` and `DestructibleValidationResults.txt`. Visual evidence is in `EntranceRevisionPreview/`.

Verified in Unity 6000.4.6f1: all 83 Play Mode destructible checks passed, including both new prop types and restoration of all eleven authored Entrance Gate props. The art authoring checks and enlarged camera renders also passed. The new sprite states are present in the multiplayer catalog; a separate live network session was not run for this art/prop revision.
